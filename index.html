Offline Usage on iPad

  1. Open the app’s GitHub Pages address in Safari on your iPad. Use the HTTPS address; opening the HTML file directly with file:// can limit service workers and PDF workers.
  2. While online, wait for the app to finish loading. The app page, scripts, local vendor/ libraries, and PDF worker are then saved in the service worker cache.
  3. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open the app once from its Home Screen icon and confirm the libraries have loaded.
  5. Turn on Airplane Mode and reopen the app to verify offline access.

After an app update, open it once while online so the new files can be cached before using it offline.
